Premium is a feeling produced by restraint and consistency, not by ornament. It
comes from a small number of decisions applied rigorously everywhere.

Levers, in order of impact: consistent spacing on a single scale; a tight type
system (one or two families, a clear scale); a restrained palette (one accent,
lots of neutral); generous whitespace; and precise, quiet detail (1px borders,
soft shadows used sparingly, aligned optical edges). Motion is subtle and
purposeful. Everything aligns to a grid; nothing is approximately placed.

Why: perceived quality tracks internal consistency. The brain reads uniform
rhythm and restrained color as "considered". Visual noise, many colors, heavy
shadows, and decorative motion read as cheap even when individually well made.

Example: a near-monochrome dashboard with one accent, 8px spacing rhythm, and a
single soft shadow tier. Counter-example: four accent colors, three shadow
depths, gradients on every card, and icons in mixed styles.
