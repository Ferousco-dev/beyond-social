On-screen captions in generated video need their own type scale, tied to the
brand system but tuned for the constraints of video, legibility over moving
footage, safe margins, a size readable at arm's length on a phone, and default
caption styling is one of the fastest ways a generated clip reads as an AI
product rather than a real creator's edit.

- Cap height around 6 to 8 percent of frame height for burned-in captions on
  vertical video; smaller reads as an afterthought, larger crowds the frame.
- Use the brand's actual UI or body sans, not a generic bold rounded "caption
  font." Most auto-caption tools default to the same two or three typefaces,
  and that default look is now recognizable and reads as templated.
- Keep a single caption weight and size for the whole clip; word-by-word size
  pumping ("karaoke" emphasis via scale jumps) is a strong AI or template
  tell, unless it's motivated by something an actual editor would do, such as
  genuinely emphasizing one word the speaker stressed.
- Position captions inside a safe margin, avoiding the bottom 12 to 15 percent
  where platform UI sits, and nudge position slightly off dead-center to avoid
  the too-perfect look of software-generated placement.

Why: viewers have absorbed thousands of hours of both real creator content and
templated AI content, and caption typography, specifically the default font,
uniform bounce-in timing, and dead-center safe-margin placement, is one of the
fastest tells separating the two. Treating captions as a designed part of the
brand's type system, rather than a tool default, is a direct lever against the
"AI look."

Example: captions in the brand's Inter Bold at 7 percent frame height, single
size throughout, positioned 18 percent from the bottom, left-aligned to match
how a speaker's own phone would actually crop.

Counter-example: default auto-caption styling, a generic rounded bold font,
dead-center placement, and per-word scale-pop emphasis on every clip, the
exact visual signature viewers now associate with mass-produced AI content.
