Natural light is the fastest way to make generated video feel real, because the
model has seen enormous amounts of it. Name the time of day and the window, and you
inherit believable, complex light for free.

- Golden hour (just after sunrise / before sunset): warm, low, long soft shadows,
  gentle rim light. The most flattering and "premium" natural look.
- Overcast: huge soft diffuser, even and shadowless, great for product and skin.
- Midday sun: hard, contrasty, unflattering overhead shadows, usually avoid for
  faces unless the harshness is the point.
- Blue hour / twilight: cool, moody, mixes with warm practical lights beautifully.
- Window light: a soft directional key indoors; place the subject beside it, not
  facing it flat-on.

Why: describing real light conditions taps the model's strongest priors and dodges
the sourceless, flat look. It is the highest quality-per-word you can spend on a
UGC or lifestyle shot.

Example: "By a large window at golden hour, warm soft light raking across the
subject's face, gentle shadows." Counter-example: "bright indoor lighting."
