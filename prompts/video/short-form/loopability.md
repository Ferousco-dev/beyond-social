Short-form platforms auto-replay. A video that loops seamlessly racks up extra
watch-time and rewatches, both of which feed reach, and often the viewer does not
notice the seam on the first pass.

How to design a loop:

- Make the last frame flow into the first: match composition, subject position,
  and motion direction so the cut from end to start is invisible.
- End on a question or a beat that the opening answers, so the second viewing feels
  intentional rather than repetitive.
- For a hard loop, literally bookend with the same shot; for a soft loop, just
  avoid a hard stop or a fade-to-black that breaks the cycle.
- Do not end on a logo card or an abrupt cut to black; those kill the loop and the
  momentum.

Why: loopability multiplies watch-time for free and is a known lever on short-form
distribution, yet most content ends flat and forfeits it.

Example: a clip that starts and ends on the same push-in toward the product, so it
cycles endlessly. Counter-example: ending on three seconds of a static logo, which
guarantees the viewer scrolls away.
