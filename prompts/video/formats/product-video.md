A short product video should make the viewer want the thing and know what it does,
fast. A reliable beat sheet from a single product photo:

1. Hook (1-2s): the most striking shot, product in motion or a satisfying detail.
2. Context (2-4s): the product in use or in an aspirational setting, showing the
   benefit, not just the object.
3. Detail (2-4s): macro shots of material, texture, and craft that justify the
   price and build desire.
4. Payoff / CTA (1-3s): the hero shot plus a clear next step or a loop back to the
   hook.

Craft notes: keep identity exact (shape, color, label), light to flatter the
material, add small motivated motion (steam, pour, rotation), and hold the grade
consistent across shots. Prefer showing the benefit to listing features.

Why: this structure covers desire (detail), relevance (context), and action (CTA)
in the seconds a feed allows, which is what converts.

Example: hero orbit -> product worn in golden-hour lifestyle -> macro of stitching
-> logo-free CTA looping to the orbit. Counter-example: 20 seconds of one slow spin
on white with a feature list.
