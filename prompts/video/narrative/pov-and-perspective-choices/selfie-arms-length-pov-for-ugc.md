The selfie, arm's-length front-camera POV has a distinct, learned visual
signature from a decade of everyday use, and getting the distance or angle
wrong is the fastest way UGC-style footage reads as a staged production
wearing a selfie costume.

- Hold the implied arm's-length distance, roughly 40-60cm, filling
  shoulders-to-crown of frame, with the faint wide-angle distortion at frame
  edges typical of a phone's front camera.
- Let the arm and framing drift slightly as if actually being held, not
  locked to a tripod-still composition.
- Allow the subject's eyeline to dip occasionally to check their own
  framing on-screen, a habit real selfie video always shows.
- Keep audio close but slightly roomy and uncompressed, not lav-mic clean
  or professionally de-noised.

Why: real selfie video's signature comes from a specific arm-extended
camera geometry and a phone mic's actual sound; any deviation, tripod-still
framing, a flattering telephoto compression, or studio-clean audio,
immediately signals a produced shoot pretending to be a selfie rather than one.

Example: "arm's-length selfie framing, slight wide-angle distortion at
edges, arm drifts naturally, audio close but roomy, not studio-clean."
Counter-example: a locked-off, telephoto-compressed close-up with pristine
lav-mic audio labeled as a selfie video; the geometry and sound both belong to a different, more produced format.
