Length is a design decision, not an afterthought. The right duration is the
shortest one that fully delivers the idea, because completion rate falls as length
grows.

Practical guidance:

- Hooks and single-idea clips: 7-15s. One punchy moment, high completion.
- Standard short-form: 15-30s. Room for hook, build, payoff without sagging.
- Deeper short-form / mini-explainers: 30-60s. Only if every second earns it.
- Ads: often 6-15s for paid placements; the first 2s must carry the brand and hook.
- Generated video clips are short by nature (a few seconds each); assemble several
  into the target length rather than expecting one long take.

Optimize for completion, not duration: a fully-watched 12s clip beats a 40s clip
abandoned at 15s, both for the viewer and for reach.

Why: platforms weight completion and rewatch heavily; padding length to seem
substantial actively hurts distribution.

Example: cut a 30s draft to 18s by removing every shot that does not advance the
story. Counter-example: stretching to 60s with slow b-roll to "look premium."
