Shoot for the ratio the platform actually shows, and compose for its safe area.

- 9:16 vertical: the default for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Fills the phone. Frame
  the subject in the central vertical band; keep essential detail out of the top
  and bottom where UI overlays sit.
- 1:1 square: safe for mixed feeds (in-feed Instagram/Facebook); wastes less on
  desktop but does not own the phone screen like 9:16.
- 16:9 horizontal: YouTube long-form, landing-page hero embeds, connected TV. Room
  for wide, cinematic composition.
- 4:5 portrait: a strong in-feed compromise on Instagram, larger than square
  without full-screen crop.

Generate at the target ratio when possible rather than cropping a 16:9 later;
center-cropping a horizontal shot to vertical usually amputates the composition.
If one master must serve several placements, compose with a protected center that
survives both a 9:16 and a 1:1 crop.

Why: the wrong ratio letterboxes or crops the subject and instantly reads as
repurposed, low-effort content on a platform that rewards native format.

Example: "vertical 9:16, subject centered in the middle third, headroom reserved
for a caption." Counter-example: a 16:9 clip posted to Reels with black bars top
and bottom.
